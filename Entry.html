Summary Formulas

The core logic behind your risk management.

  • Risk per Trade = Account Balance × Risk %
  • Stop-Loss = Support Level - Buffer
  • Position Size = Risk per Trade ÷ (Entry Price - Stop Loss)
  • Reward:Risk = (Target Price - Entry) ÷ (Entry - Stop Loss)
